# Sample licenses

All audio in `../library/*.mp3` is derived from sources dedicated to the public
domain under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal**. The original source notices
are preserved alongside this file. This applies to the audio samples, not to
the application code, third-party software or generated instrument artwork.

- FreePats: `piano.txt`, `guitar.txt`, `electric-guitar.txt`, `finger-bass.txt`,
  `fm-piano.txt`, `accordion.txt`, `organ.txt`, `ukulele.txt`, `ocarina.txt`,
  `square.txt`, `synth-bass.txt`, `lead.txt`, `warm-pad.txt`, `choir-pad.txt`,
  `bagpipe.txt`, `jaw-harp.txt`, `world-percussion.txt`.
- Versilian Studios Community Sample Library: `VCSL.txt`.
- Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition: `VSCO-2-CE.txt`.
- Meta Open Sound Steel Guitar: `steel-guitar.txt`.

`electronic-drums.txt` documents an investigated CC0 bank retained in the source
lock; the final acoustic percussion kit uses VCSL. Source URLs, archive/input
SHA-256, revisions, selected recordings, pitch changes, EQ and output SHA-256
are in `../library/manifest.json`. Copies of the notices are supplied for
transparency even where attribution is not required by CC0.

CC0 text and explanation: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.

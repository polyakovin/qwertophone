Wooden Frog Guiro
Recorded by Skybonator, published May 29, 2024.

The author describes a hollow wooden frog with notches, scraped with a stick,
recorded with a TASCAM DX-05X and TAKSTAR external condenser microphone.

Qwertophone uses the publicly available HQ MP3 preview as its source, rather
than the original stereo WAV. The first isolated scrape is extracted, mixed
to mono, trimmed, normalized and faded without transposition or looping.
The source URL, SHA-256 and processing parameters are fixed in
scripts/audio/one-shots.sources.json; the delivered file's checksum and frame
count are in public/samples/library/one-shots/manifest.json.
